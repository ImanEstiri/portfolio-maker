# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and give them the benefit of
the doubt you would want.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Review the code, not the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and learn from them rather than defending them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report to **konradcinkusz@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer will respond as promptly as a personal project allows, and may remove
comments, reject contributions, or block accounts that breach this document.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
